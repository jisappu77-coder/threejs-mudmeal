OpenStreetMap API and distributed under the Open Data Commons Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Attribution and contributor details: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Each JSON records its source query, bounds, original OSM way IDs and fetch date.
The publicly accessible JSON files are the downloadable derived databases.

No map tiles, satellite photography or proprietary map-provider assets were used.
Procedural terrain heights, vegetation, building heights where absent from OSM,
vehicle traffic and scenery are illustrative game content, not surveyed data.

munnar-adventure.json includes fictional game trails and OSM-derived paved-road
connections; the combined database is also distributed under ODbL 1.0.
