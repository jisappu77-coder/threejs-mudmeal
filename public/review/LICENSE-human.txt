Each selected OBJ, mhclo and material header explicitly releases the asset
under CC0 in September 2020, with Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas
Hauquier listed as the copyright holders at release.

Selected assets:
- clothes/male_casualsuit01: authored collared shirt, jeans and belt;
  diffuse, normal and ambient-occlusion maps.
- clothes/shoes01: authored leather shoes and socks; diffuse and normal maps.
- hair/short02: authored strand cards and alpha colour atlas, fitted through the source proxy mappings, preserving authored strand layers.
- eyebrows/eyebrow001: authored tapered eyebrow cards and alpha colour atlas.
- eyes/high-poly and eyes/materials/brown_eye.png: anatomical eyes,
  authored iris/sclera colour map and transparent cornea region.
- skins/young_african_male/young_darkskinned_male_diffuse.png:
  authored UV skin colour atlas. Texture selection is not a claim of regional identity.

Modifications: fitted to the adult body through the authored proxy mappings,
posed with lowered arms and slightly staggered/outward feet; concealed body
faces removed using garment delete masks; shirt and jeans separated into
objects; surfaces subdivided; scaled to 1.75 metres; colour maps compressed,
normal maps resized; iris colour graded to less saturated dark brown.
Concealed upper sock shafts removed after close-up clipping review.
The browser darkens the hair material, uses alpha cutouts for strands and brows,
and slightly warms the sclera. The original strand topology is preserved. GLB contains the meshes and textures.
Rebuild instructions: scripts/build-human.py (Blender 4.0).
