# Original motorcycle models

The game offers four fictional designs: **City 125** (angular commuter), **Heritage 350** (classic roadster), **Daily 110** (slim everyday commuter), and **Metro 110** (step-through scooter). Choose one under **Explore Kerala → Bike style**.

Their meshes are generated by project-authored Three.js code in `src/prototypes/vehicles.js`. They use original silhouettes, neutral names, procedural materials and the game's own delivery branding. No manufacturer CAD, downloaded motorcycle models, product photographs, badges, logos or branded liveries were used. They are not official Honda, Royal Enfield or Hero models or exact replicas of their products.

The variants share rider contact points so switching bikes preserves the human riding pose. Wheel animation and collision bounds update with the selected chassis. The existing human models retain their separate CC0 provenance in `public/characters/LICENSE.txt`.
