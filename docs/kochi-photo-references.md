# Kochi and Kerala photo references

Collected on 2026-10-04 in response to the request to use real photographs of houses, props and objects. The local photo board is `artifacts/kochi-photo-references/index.html`; each card links to its original publisher and a matching Google Images query. Google automated search returned a JavaScript/browser challenge, so the inspected photographs were fetched directly from Kerala Tourism. Photo copies and the board remain in ignored local artifacts; the game does not load them.

## Art pass informed by the photographs

1. Model roof construction: tile rows, deeper overhangs, visible underside rafters, fascia, gutters and downpipes. Avoid treating a roof as a smooth orange cap.
2. Give buildings useful entrances and individual frontage: veranda posts and steps, window sunshades, an occasional outside staircase, compound wall and gate. Keep the mapped footprint and safe road clearance.
3. Dress selected shopfronts with a small, coherent display of original vessels, lamps, shelves and tables; model pots, wall wiring and drainage where appropriate. Avoid identical props on every building.
4. Replace broad bare grass around urban buildings with varied frontage surfaces where appropriate: paved strips, courtyard earth, planted edges and boundary walls. Keep existing geographic water and roads.
5. Keep a mixture of modern urban buildings, ordinary homes, shops and a smaller number of heritage buildings. The active geographic map is Ernakulam; these Fort Kochi and Palakkad photographs are regional references, not evidence that every Divans Road building should have heritage architecture.
6. Keep the user's warm, vibrant Mud Meals palette. Read construction, proportions and materials from the photographs; their individual lighting and camera colour casts are not the game grading target.

The approved references now inform the Kochi art pass: roof tiles follow each roof face, deeper eaves expose timber rafters, and gutters connect to downpipes. Homes receive sunshades, paneled doors, entrance steps, garden pots and, where room allows, courtyard surfaces, open compound-wall entrances and occasional outside stairs. Selected shops have timber shelves with original brass vessels and hanging lamps. Ground objects have collision footprints and are placed clear of mapped roads, buildings, water and existing tree trunks. These details reuse the existing instanced rendering path and keep the supplied game palette.

Models and texture treatments are original constructions informed by the observed features. No reuse rights for the source photographs have been established, so they are not production textures or bundled game assets. The source photographs of specific landmarks do not mean that a landmark has been added to the Ernakulam snapshot.
